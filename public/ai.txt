# AI.TXT - Machine-Consumable Information

## Tool: Mortgage Calculator

### Description
A web-based mortgage calculator for calculating monthly payments, total interest, and payment breakdowns.

### Access
- URL: /
- Type: Web application
- Authentication: None required

### Capabilities
- Calculate monthly mortgage payments
- Calculate total interest and total payment
- Visualize payment breakdown
- Support various loan terms and rates

### Inputs
- Home price
- Down payment
- Loan term (years)
- Interest rate (percentage)

### Outputs
- Monthly payment
- Total payment
- Total interest
- Payment breakdown chart

### Technical
- Client-side calculations
- No external dependencies
- Mobile-responsive
- No data collection
